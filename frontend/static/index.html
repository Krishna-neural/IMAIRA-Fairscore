IMAIRA FairScore

"Grade what you know, not how you sound."

An intelligent, accent-aware technical interview evaluation platform that recognizes technical terminology, verifies speech-to-text acoustic confusions, and evaluates candidates through three strictly independent performance dimensions.

📉

Conventional ASR Bias

Standard speech-to-text models systematically mistranscribe non-native English accents and Indian-English phonetic patterns, corrupting critical technical terms ("has map" for "hash map").

🛡️

Stage 2 Terminology Verification

IMAIRA FairScore indexes 2,000+ technical engineering terms with RapidFuzz and Metaphone phonetic matching, re-verifying suspect spans against audio slices with conservative abstention.

📐

Independent Dimensions

Content mastery, structural organization, and verbal fluency are evaluated through independent calibrated engines. Accent and L2 syntax never penalize conceptual scores.

Core Evaluation Architecture

Three Independent Assessment Dimensions

Unlike monolithic AI interview bots that blend grammar with technical competence, IMAIRA FairScore decouples evaluation into three orthogonal criteria:

Dimension 1 • 50% Weight

Content Mastery

Evaluates underlying cause-and-effect, architectural principles, and conceptual depth against semantic rubric propositions (≥5 words).

✅ Strictly tolerates ASR errors, code-mixed fillers, and L2 syntax.
Dimension 2 • 30% Weight

Structure & Coherence

Analyzes argument progression: Framing (35% - opening directness), Coherence (40% - causal sequence), and Closure (25% - definitive summary).

✅ Decoupled from factual correctness and pronunciation.
Dimension 3 • 20% Weight

Communication Fluency

Evaluates delivery pace (ideal 110-170 WPM), filler word density, restart frequency, and extended hesitation pauses (>2s).

✅ Never penalizes accent variation or localized vocabulary.

Technology & Model Disclosures

Pretrained Models:

Faster-Whisper large-v3 (CTranslate2 backend, int8_float16 quantization), Google Gemini 2.5 Flash for semantic rubric proposition tagging, Isotonic Regression for score calibration.

Phonetic & Lexical Matching Libraries:

RapidFuzz (Levenshtein ratio and token sort ratio), Jellyfish (Metaphone and Soundex phonetic hash encoding).

Datasets & Glossaries:

2,000-term technical engineering glossary (data/glossary.csv), 120 validated technical question bank (data/QandA.json), human-calibrated rating reports (reports/scorer_robustness_report.json).

Ethical & Fairness Standards:

Adheres strictly to Integraminds AI Frontier fairness mandates: zero accent discrimination, decoupled scoring dimensions, and transparent audit logging.
