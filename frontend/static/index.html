The Evaluation Problem

Your Accent Shouldn't Define Your Technical Ability.

Standard speech recognition models systematically fail on regional accents and code-mixed speech, corrupting technical keywords and penalizing qualified candidates.

Conventional Speech-to-Text
Acoustic Confusion & Unfair Penalties

Generic ASR models mishear non-native pronunciations of technical terms. The downstream evaluation bot interprets acoustic transcription errors as candidate ignorance.

Raw Candidate Utterance: "We store user profiles in a hash map..."
Conventional ASR: "We store user profiles in a has map..."
Evaluation Result: Deducted 15 marks for missing key data structure concept.
IMAIRA FairScore Stage 2 Pipeline
Evidence-Checked Terminology Recovery

IMAIRA indexes 2,000+ technical terms, detects suspect spans, and acoustically verifies candidate phonemes. Valid concepts are recovered while ambiguous terms trigger conservative abstention.

Engineering Architecture

An Evaluation Pipeline Built Around Fairness.

Five coordinated processing stages ensure objective, repeatable technical evaluations independent of acoustic surface variations.

01
Capture
Web Audio API captures continuous candidate speech with real-time audio volume analysis and client-side privacy isolation.
02
Transcribe
Streaming ASR engine delivers sub-second provisional transcripts, updating continuous hypotheses as candidates formulate answers.
03
Verify
Stage 2 span detector scans recognized text against 2,000 glossary terms, recovering phonetically confused technical tokens.
04
Evaluate
Decoupled scoring assesses Content Mastery (50%), Structure & Coherence (30%), and Communication Fluency (20%).
05
Report
Isotonic calibrators normalize scores, generate semantic rubric coverage badges, and surface top-3 coaching fixes.
Decoupled Evaluation Criteria

Three Independent Assessment Dimensions

Unlike monolithic AI interviewers that conflate grammar with engineering capability, IMAIRA FairScore measures each dimension using isolated, calibrated models.

Dimension 1 • 50% Weight
Content Mastery

Evaluates factual engineering accuracy, underlying cause-and-effect reasoning, and architectural completeness against semantic rubric propositions (≥5 words).

Fairness Invariant: Strictly invariant to surface vocabulary, speech pauses, and minor grammar variations.
Dimension 2 • 30% Weight
Structure & Coherence

Evaluates logical organization across three sub-dimensions: Framing (35% - opening directness), Coherence (40% - causal argument flow), and Closure (25% - definitive synthesis).

Fairness Invariant: Completely decoupled from pronunciation, accent cadence, and acoustic pitch.
Dimension 3 • 20% Weight
Communication Fluency

Evaluates delivery pace (optimal 110–170 WPM), filler word frequency ("um", "like", "you know"), and extended hesitation pauses (>2.5s).

Fairness Invariant: Focuses strictly on conversational flow without penalizing regional intonation.

Technology & Model Disclosures

Pretrained Models:

Faster-Whisper large-v3 (CTranslate2 backend, int8_float16 quantization), Google Gemini 2.5 Flash for semantic rubric proposition tagging, Isotonic Regression for score calibration.

Phonetic & Lexical Matching Libraries:

RapidFuzz (Levenshtein ratio and token sort ratio), Jellyfish (Metaphone and Soundex phonetic hash encoding).

Datasets & Glossaries:

2,000-term technical engineering glossary (data/glossary.csv), 120 validated technical question bank (data/QandA.json), human-calibrated rating reports (reports/scorer_robustness_report.json).

Ethical & Fairness Standards:

Adheres strictly to Integraminds AI Frontier fairness mandates: zero accent discrimination, decoupled scoring dimensions, and transparent audit logging.
